Cancel Netflix

Difficulty easy · 22/100 Time ~90 seconds Last verified May 12, 2026 Refund none — access until period ends

Netflix is one of the more honest cancellations on the internet. No retention pop-ups, no phone calls, no hidden screens. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period.

Go to Netflix
step 01

Sign in on the web

Open netflix.com/login in any browser and sign in. Do not use the mobile app — on iOS, Netflix will redirect you to Apple Subscriptions instead of handling the cancellation itself.

screenshot — netflix sign-in screen
step 02

Open Account → Membership

Click your profile icon in the top-right corner, then choose Account. Under the Membership & Billing section, press Cancel Membership.

Netflix may offer to downgrade your plan instead. Ignore — proceed to Cancel.
step 03

Confirm the cancellation

You'll see the date your access ends. No three-screen "are you sure?" — one click confirms. Netflix will email you a confirmation within five minutes.

Cancel Adobe Creative Cloud

Difficulty hard · 94/100 Time ~9 minutes Last verified May 8, 2026 Refund partial — see step 5

Adobe is the most resistant subscription we track. The cancel flow is hidden three menus deep, the early-termination fee is large, and the retention page will try to keep you with a "free month" offer twice.

Heads up. If you're on an annual plan and cancel before month 14, Adobe charges 50% of the remaining contract as an early termination fee. Cancel within 14 days of signing up for a full refund.
Go to Adobe Account
step 01

Sign in to your Adobe account

Open account.adobe.com in a desktop browser. The cancel option is not exposed in the Creative Cloud desktop app.

screenshot — adobe account dashboard
step 02

Open Plans & Payment → Manage plan

Click your active subscription. Scroll past the upsell banner and find the tiny Manage plan link below the renewal date.

step 03

Pick "Cancel your plan"

This is on a separate screen from "change your plan". Adobe sometimes hides it behind a ? icon — look in the lower right of the plan box.

If you don't see "Cancel your plan", you're on a team plan. The owner of the team must cancel from the Admin Console.
step 04

Decline the retention offers

Adobe will offer two months free, then a 50% discount, then a downgrade to Photography plan. Decline each one — there is no penalty.

step 05

Review the termination fee, then confirm

If you're past day 14 of an annual plan, Adobe shows the fee here. You can still proceed — the fee will be billed within 24 hours. Click Confirm cancellation.

If you contest the fee, contact Adobe support and reference the 14-day refund policy on annual plans. They will sometimes waive it.

Cancel Noom

Difficulty hard · 88/100 Time ~7 minutes Last verified May 4, 2026

Noom routes you through a chatbot, then a survey, then three retention screens before letting you cancel. We walk you through every screen.

Go to Noom Account
step 01

Open the chatbot

Sign in at noom.com, then under Settings tap Cancel my subscription. You'll be redirected to a chatbot — this is the only working path.

step 02

Type "cancel" three times

The bot will rephrase your request as questions. Stay on message: type cancel, then I'm sure, then continue.

step 03

Skip the exit survey

It's optional. Scroll to the bottom of the survey screen and tap the small grey Skip link.

step 04

Decline retention offers

Noom offers a one-month pause, then 50% off, then a longer trial. Decline all three.

step 05

Get the confirmation email

If you don't receive an email within 24 hours, your cancellation didn't go through — check your account or repeat from step 1.

Cancel Amazon Prime

Difficulty medium · 71/100 Time ~5 minutes Last verified May 10, 2026

Amazon's flow is the textbook example of a confirmation-shaming dark pattern. The buttons are deliberately mis-labelled to make ending the membership feel like a mistake.

step 01

Open Your Account → Prime Membership

Sign in at amazon.com, hover Account & Lists, choose Prime Membership.

step 02

Click "Manage Membership" → "End Membership"

Look for the small dropdown on the right side. The link is grey and easy to miss.

step 03

Click "End Membership" — three times

Amazon shows the same screen three times in a row, each with a slightly different button text. Watch carefully — the prominent button always continues your membership.

On each screen, the cancellation link is the grey, smaller text. The bright yellow button keeps you subscribed.
step 04

Choose "End on [date]"

Pick to keep access until the renewal date — there's no benefit to immediate cancellation unless you want a partial refund.

About

CancelHub started as a frustration. Adobe was demanding a phone call. Noom had hidden the cancel link behind three "are you sure?" screens. The web is full of "best ways to cancel X" articles that turn out to be ad farms with the wrong instructions.
