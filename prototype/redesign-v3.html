The trap.

Adobe Creative Cloud is, by every objective measure, the hardest mainstream subscription to leave on the consumer internet. We have been timing cancellation flows for 16 months and Adobe has held the top spot in 14 of them.

The mechanism is layered. Each screen in the flow does a different job. The plans page hides the cancel link below the fold and renders it as grey 10-pixel text — the visual hierarchy makes you reach for the storage upgrade instead. The plan management page puts the cancel option behind a ? icon, which is the universal symbol for "help", not "destructive action".

"No thanks, continue cancelling" — Adobe, three times in a row, in 10-pixel grey.

Then come the three retention screens. Two months free. Fifty percent off. A downgrade to the Photography plan. Each of them has a prominent primary button that keeps you paying, and a tiny grey link that lets you proceed. The primary button changes label each time so it's harder to muscle-memory through.

The last screen is the fee. $329.96 in our case — half of what we still owed on the annual plan. The fee was never shown when we signed up. It surfaces here, two clicks before the end, in red on red. There is no "wait, let me reconsider" path that doesn't restart the entire flow.

The escape.

If you want to skip the analysis and just leave: the exact sequence of clicks, ignoring every retention dialogue.

  1. 01Open account.adobe.com on a desktop browser. The Creative Cloud desktop app cannot cancel.
  2. 02Scroll past the storage upsell, the Firefly card, and the changelog. Click the grey Manage plan link in the lower right.
  3. 03Click the ? icon in the upper right of your active plan card. Choose Cancel your plan.
  4. 04Decline three retention offers. The "continue cancelling" link is the grey one, always.
  5. 05Review the early-termination fee. If you signed up more than 14 days ago, this fee applies. Click Confirm cancellation.
  6. 06Save the confirmation email — Adobe support has been known to "lose" cancellation records.

The receipt.

We started the timer at 14:02 EST. The first retention screen appeared at 14:08. The fee disclosure surfaced at 14:18. Cancellation confirmed at 14:21. Total elapsed: 18 minutes 42 seconds. Cost: $329.96. Bonus: Adobe emailed us a "we miss you already" newsletter 17 minutes later, offering 50% off.
